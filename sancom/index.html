Programming Principles for Sandhurst Athletes
Strength work must complement — not compete with — your existing run, ruck and WOD volume. Priority is always the PTI-scheduled sessions.
Schedule strength sessions Tuesday (Session A) and Thursday morning (Session B). Thursday strength must be completed before VDOT intervals — keep it upper body or posterior chain focused to avoid lower body fatigue before intervals.
Never do heavy lower body strength (heavy squats, leg press) on Thursday — VDOT intervals follow. Saturday pack march means Friday should also avoid heavy lower body. Upper body and posterior chain are the safe choices mid-week.
Posterior chain strength (hinge, glute, hamstring) transfers directly to ruck performance. Prioritise this over quad-dominant work.
Grip strength is a Sandhurst-specific limiter — farmer's carries, dead hangs, and towel pull-ups should feature every cycle.
Core anti-rotation and anti-extension (plank variants, Pallof press, dead bug) build the stability needed for loaded tasks under fatigue.
Track your main lift numbers. Add load only when you complete all reps and sets with clean technique. Quality reps beat max effort ego lifts.
Deload every 4–6 weeks: drop to 60% load, focus on technique. This prevents accumulated fatigue from stacking on top of your run/ruck volume.
